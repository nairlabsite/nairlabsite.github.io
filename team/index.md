## Join Our Team

The Nair Laboratory is growing and welcomes inquiries from motivated students interested in cancer, epigenetics, cell migration, and metastasis. As an early member of the lab, students will have opportunities to work closely with Prof. Nair, develop technical and quantitative research skills, and contribute to new research directions. 

Our research integrates cancer bioengineering, molecular and cellular biology, bioinformatics, imaging, and animal models of cancer progression and metastasis. Students can become involved in ongoing projects while developing skills in experimental design, data analysis, scientific communication, and laboratory research.

### Graduate Students

Prospective Master's and PhD students interested in joining the lab are encouraged to contact Prof. Nair to discuss research opportunities, graduate programs, and the application process.

### Undergraduate Students

Undergraduate students interested in research opportunities are encouraged to contact Prof. Nair or stop by his office to discuss current projects and opportunities to become involved in the lab.
