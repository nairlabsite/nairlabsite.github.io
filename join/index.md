# Join Us!

## Fully Funded PhD Opportunity in Cancer Metastasis, Epigenetics, and Biophysics — Fall 2027

The Nair Laboratory at William & Mary is seeking motivated students for a fully funded PhD opportunity in the Applied Science PhD Program, beginning Fall 2027. We are interested in students who want to uncover the molecular and epigenetic mechanisms that drive cancer progression and metastasis.

Our research focuses on understanding how epigenetic regulation and cellular biophysics control cancer cell migration, invasion, and metastatic progression. We integrate approaches from cancer biology, epigenetics, molecular and cellular biology, animal models, bioinformatics, and biophysics to discover and characterize novel regulators of metastasis.

Potential research areas include:

- Epigenetic regulation of cancer metastasis
- Mechanisms of cancer cell migration and invasion
- Identification and functional validation of metastatic drivers
- Biophysical regulation of cancer progression

Students will have opportunities to develop independent research projects, gain experience with diverse experimental and computational approaches, present their work at scientific conferences, and contribute to peer-reviewed publications while working closely with the PI in a growing research laboratory.

Applicants with backgrounds in biology, bioengineering, molecular and cellular biology, biochemistry, genetics, biomedical sciences, or related fields who are interested in joining the Applied Science PhD Program in Fall 2027 are encouraged to inquire.

Interested candidates should contact **Prof. Praful Nair at pnair@wm.edu** with a CV and a brief description of their research background, interests, and reasons for pursuing a PhD in the Nair Laboratory.



## Other Opportunities

The Nair Laboratory is growing and welcomes motivated undergraduate and graduate students interested in cancer biology, epigenetics, cell migration, and metastasis.

Please email **Prof. Praful Nair at pnair@wm.edu** or stop by **ISC3, Room 0279**. When contacting the lab, please include your CV or résumé along with a brief description of your research interests and background.
