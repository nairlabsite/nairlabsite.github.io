# Join Us!

The Nair Laboratory is growing and welcomes motivated undergraduate and graduate students interested in cancer biology, epigenetics, cell migration, and metastasis.

Please email **Prof. Praful Nair at pnair@wm.edu** or stop by **ISC3, Room 0279**. When contacting the lab, please include your CV or résumé along with a brief description of your research interests and background.
